## Dataset Description

This repository contains the **raw data** used in the study:  
**Souza Holanda, P.M., de Souza, J.L.P., & Baccaro, F.B. (2021)**  
_Seasonal fluctuation of groundwater level influences local litter‐dwelling ant richness, composition, and colonization in the Amazon rainforest._  
*Ecological Entomology*. [https://doi.org/10.1111/een.12954](https://doi.org/10.1111/een.12954)

---

### Study Overview

The study investigates how **seasonal fluctuations in groundwater level** affect **litter-dwelling ant communities** in riparian forests of Central Amazonia. By comparing ant diversity, dominance, and colonization patterns between the **rainy (shallow water-table)** and **dry (deep water-table)** seasons, it tests how these natural disturbances influence biodiversity and community assembly processes.

---

### Data Content

The Excel file in this repository includes raw data for:

- **Ant species composition and abundance**  
  – From leaf litter and litter accumulated at the base of stemless palms (*Attalea microcarpa*)  
  – Sampled in **10 plots** during **rainy and dry seasons**

- **Dominance and bait experiments**  
  – Ant occurrence and bait monopolization (used to infer dominance hierarchy)

- **Colonization experiment**  
  – Number and identity of ant colonies established in **artificial litter patches** over 6 and 12 months  
  – Differentiated by microhabitat (leaf litter vs. palm base) and seasonality

Each sheet or table is organized by **plot ID**, **season**, **habitat type**, and various sampling attributes (e.g., abundance, richness, or colony count), matching the methodologies described in the paper.

---

### Study Location

All data were collected in the **Reserva Florestal Adolpho Ducke**, a protected forest near Manaus, Brazil, monitored by the **PPBio** biodiversity research program.
